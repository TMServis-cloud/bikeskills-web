Camps and workshops

Weekend or one-day camps and workshops for everyone who wants to learn basic and advanced technique, ride with a great group of people and enjoy everything the bike has to offer. A workshop can also be an attractive side programme for your event – spectators learn something and can even have a go themselves.

about the camps

Every year we plan only a limited number of camps, and we intend to enjoy them to the full. The focus is always the same – technique – but the difficulty level varies. We're not big travellers, so most camps take place in Central Bohemia for smaller groups of riders we can fully dedicate ourselves to. Your time and ours is precious, so our camps last two days at most and the coaching is designed accordingly.

FIND YOUR CAMP

instructors

The camps are led by the main instructors from our TEAM, as well as advanced members of our club MTB & TRIAL Mraveniště Říčany, who have many years of racing experience in various disciplines themselves. We have plenty to say about technique, and speed is no stranger to any of our members either. Enduro, DH, XC, Trial – we've ridden it all, even though each of us has a different main discipline.

safety

Participation in a camp is always at your own risk. Every participant must wear a helmet and sturdy shoes; protectors are optional. Each rider is responsible for the technical condition of their own bike. Please inform the instructors in advance about any health issues. The briefing includes a short safety talk. First aid will be within driving distance of the camp, or a medic will be present on site.

PROGRAMME?

Welcome / breakfast, a chat with participants, warm-up, a bit of theory with demonstrations, splitting into groups if needed – and then we get to it. After a few hours of training we have lunch together, go through what you've learned and choose the next focus of the coaching and our rides. To finish, you can join us for dinner with a camp review and our final tips for you.

how to sign up?

Once you have chosen a camp, sign up using the registration form. Alternatively, email us at info@bikeskills.cz, or call +420 608 959 098. A camp can also be given as a gift voucher.

about the workshops

Our team of instructors has more than 30 years on the bike. We have raced at home and around the world in Elite Trial categories, organised many sports and social events, we are ambassadors of several cycling projects, ride for a number of leading brands on the Czech market and work with TV shows focused on cycling. We will gladly bring all this experience to your event in the form of an educational workshop.

We provide complete facilities – from an obstacle course (local terrain can also be used) to a sound system, hosts, tents, Trialshow obstacles and bikes to borrow.

We can also put on a small exhibition on mountain bikes / e-bikes, or a full-scale Trial show on trial bikes, which can be combined with a moto-trial show, parkour or unicycling.
